Our Mission

Our mission is to revolutionize accessibility and personalization in prosthetic ear design through cutting-edge 3D printing technology. We strive to provide affordable, accessible, and aesthetically realistic solutions for individuals with microtia.



1 in 6,000 people live with microtia, a congenital condition where the external ear is absent or underdeveloped. By leveraging innovative materials and user-centered design, we aim to make reconstructive technology accessible to the entire world.


About Aurius
The Cost Problem

Bringing it within reach.

A traditional prosthetic ear costs between $4,000 and $8,000 and is rarely covered by insurance. Our goal is to change that — without compromising on quality, comfort, or aesthetics.

Step-By-Step Process

From scan to finished prosthetic.

Step 01

LiDAR Technologies

Using LiDAR technology, we capture highly detailed 3D renders of the ear. Scans are meticulously examined to isolate ear geometry, ensuring precise anatomical fidelity for custom prosthetic modeling.

Step 02

3D Render and Design

LiDAR scans are exported as STL files and refined using Fusion 360 and MeshMixer. Surface imperfections are repaired and the model is transformed into a print-ready, high-resolution digital file.

Step 03

Printing and Manufacturing

Models are printed on widely-available commercial 3D printers using flexible, prosthetic-grade TPU filament for lifelike, soft-touch results that mimic the natural feel of the human ear.

By leveraging innovative materials and user-centered design, we bridge the gap between medical necessity and personal identity — ensuring everyone has access to high-quality, customizable prosthetics.

Who We Are — Innovate and Empower.

Aurius Prosthetics is an open-source assistive technology organization focused on redefining accessibility through innovation.

Our initiative is rooted in the belief that high-quality prosthetic care should be universally accessible — not limited by cost or geography.

Founded by King Oung, Aurius develops customizable, 3D-printed prosthetic ear solutions that are affordable, accessible, and freely available to individuals around the world — particularly those affected by microtia and related conditions.

We are actively collaborating with medical professionals around the world — such as Dr. Zung-Chung Chen, a world-renowned expert in craniofacial reconstruction and a leading authority in microtia surgery — to help Aurius grow and scale its global impact. These partnerships not only refine our designs, but also support awareness efforts and help deliver our prosthetic solutions to the communities that need them most.

A 3D-printed ear designed for accessibility, dignity, and adaptability.

This model is part of our ongoing mission to provide customizable, low-cost prosthetics for those in need. Printed in skin-tone TPU filament, it achieves a lifelike flexibility and soft-touch quality that rigid materials cannot.
